# FirstRemote BVN workflow reference

Source: `FirstRemote_BVN_Pitch_Deck_Complete.pdf` supplied for this prototype.

## Director journey (nine displayed stages; PDF pages 5–8)
1. Invitation — single-use English/Mandarin link from the Chinese Desk RM.
2. Consent — NDPA biometric consent.
3. Passport — e-passport NFC chip read and MRZ check.
4. Face match — liveness selfie compared with chip photo.
5. Fingerprints — camera-based contactless capture to NIBSS standard; phone's built-in fingerprint sensor is not used.
6. Screening — sanctions, PEP and adverse-media checks.
7. Approval — maker-checker review of evidence pack by Business Unit.
8. Submission — through the approved BVN enrolment channel to NIBSS.
9. BVN issued — validated issuance by NIBSS.

## 90-day control proposal (PDF pages 9–12)
- Day 0: account opens on deferral and invitation is sent.
- Day 15: first reminder to director and RM.
- Day 30: uncured account flagged on RM dashboard.
- Day 60: escalation to RM line manager.
- Day 75: final warning and fast-track review.
- Day 90: automatic restriction; CEO extension only as a logged exception.

The deck states that reminder days, limits, the 90-day deferral policy, and extension rules require FirstBank Compliance and Legal approval. NIBSS/CBN approvals and approved identity, biometric, screening, cloud and legal partners are dependencies. All screens in this prototype are simulations; no live biometric, NIBSS, World-Check, CRM or core-banking integration is present.
